Who we are

About SDARIM

Somali Disability Association Refugee in Malaysia (SDARIM) is a community-based, non-profit initiative supporting refugee persons with disabilities, including children and adults, and their families.

Founded in July 2020, SDARIM works to promote dignity, inclusion, protection, education, and access to essential support for people who may face barriers because of disability and displacement.

Our services and activities depend on available resources and partnerships. Please contact us to discuss current support and visiting arrangements.
